# 0006 · Dated history, corrections in place, locked months — 2026-10-09

**Decision:** every RM / share / branch change has a "from" date and keeps the old value for earlier months. A change dated
on the entry's own first day corrects it (audited "CORRECT"). FINANCE locks a month; nothing dated may reach into it; a
corrected file for a locked month posts the difference into the next open month.

**Why:** reports for a past month must never move once finance has signed them; mistakes are still fixable without
rewriting history.

**Rules:** R-40 … R-44.
