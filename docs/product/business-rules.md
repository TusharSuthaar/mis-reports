# Business rules

Every rule the system applies to money or to who-gets-what. **Each row names the test that proves it** — `GuardrailsTest`
fails the build if a named test does not exist. Changing a rule = change the row, the code and the test together.

## Who a client is

| ID | Rule | Example | Proved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | A client is one **PAN**. Several Finesse codes of one person add up into one row. | Two accounts of the same PAN → one line, amounts added | `UploadIT.codesFindTheirClientByPanOrGuardianPanAndTheRestBlockProcess` |
| R-02 | A client **with its own PAN** is mapped only by that PAN. If that PAN is not in Master MIS it is **highlighted**, even if the guardian's PAN or the name is there. | Minor with own PAN not in master → highlighted | `UploadIT.codesFindTheirClientByPanOrGuardianPanAndTheRestBlockProcess`, `UploadIT.aClientWithoutPanIsMatchedByItsExactNameInMasterMis` |
| R-03 | A client **without a PAN** is mapped by its **guardian's PAN**; failing that, by its exact name among Master MIS clients that have no PAN (only if one such client). | Minor without PAN → guardian's RM and partners | `UploadIT.aClientWithoutPanIsMatchedByItsExactNameInMasterMis` |
| R-04 | A PAN is stored encrypted. It can be searched in full and corrected only through the app (audited; refused if another client has it, unless that other record has never had an RM — a duplicate a file created — and the user asks to move the PAN). A minor's record carrying the guardian's PAN (the guardian's record has it too) can give it up; the minor then maps by guardian PAN (R-03). | Search "abcde1234f" finds the client | `ClientPanIT.aPanIsCorrectedAndAudited`, `ClientPanIT.aClientIsFoundByItsFullPanInAnyCase`, `ClientPanIT.aPanOnADuplicateWithoutRmIsMovedToTheRightClient`, `ClientPanIT.aMinorsRecordCarryingTheGuardiansPanGivesItUp` |
| R-05 | The **Clients screen** is where client data is kept up to date: add a client by hand (PAN must be new; RM from a date), correct or move a PAN, mark a guardian's PAN, change RM, set partners and %s. Lists show what needs attention: PANs on more than one record, clients without RM, clients without PAN. Every change is audited and shown in the client's history. | New client → added with RM from 1 Sep | `ClientPanIT.aClientIsAddedByHandWithItsRmAndAudited`, `ClientPanIT.needsAttentionListsFindSharedPansAndClientsWithoutPan` |
| R-06 | A PAN corrected on the Clients screen is remembered as the client's **former PAN** (hash only): a later Master MIS or Finesse file that still carries the old PAN finds that client instead of adding a new one, as long as no client holds that PAN now. | Master typo ABCDE1234X fixed to ABCDE1234F; next master still says …X → same client | `ShareIT.aPanCorrectedOnTheClientsScreenStillMatchesAMasterWithTheOldPan` |
| R-07 | Two records of one person are **merged** on the Clients screen: one is removed, its codes, unsaved upload rows and former PANs move to the other, and its partners too when the other has none; the kept record keeps its RM history and takes the PAN chosen; the other PAN becomes a former PAN (R-06). Refused for a record with saved brokerage. Audited. | Finesse PAN with swapped digits made a second record → merge, keep the right PAN | `ClientPanIT.twoRecordsOfOnePersonAreMergedKeepingTheChosenPan` |

## Changes over time and month closing

| ID | Rule | Example | Proved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-40 | RM and sharing changes are dated. A change from a later date keeps earlier months as they were; a change from the entry's own first day **corrects** it (audited). | 50% from Aug, 70% from Oct → Aug–Sep stay 50% | `ShareIT.aClientsPercentIsChangedFromADateOrCorrectedFromItsFirstDay`, `TimelineTest.historyIsNotRewritten` |
| R-41 | Month status: OPEN → PROCESSED → LOCKED. A dated change reaching a processed month reopens it. | Change from 1-Aug → Aug back to OPEN | `MonthLifecycleIT.saveProcessLockAndCorrect` |
| R-42 | Only FINANCE locks; only ADMIN unlocks, with a reason. No dated change may reach into a locked month. | HO cannot lock | `MonthLifecycleIT.saveProcessLockAndCorrect` |
| R-43 | A corrected file for a locked month changes nothing in it: the difference per client is posted as an adjustment in the next open month, split as the locked month stood. | +10 in Aug (locked) → adjustment in Sep | `MonthLifecycleIT.saveProcessLockAndCorrect` |
| R-44 | Ledger lines and splits are append-only (voided, never changed or deleted). | UPDATE refused by the database | `UploadIT.ledgerLinesCannotBeChangedOrDeleted`, `MonthLifecycleIT.splitsCannotBeChangedOrDeleted` |
| R-45 | Imports, loads and processing run **one at a time** per kind: a second one is refused while the first is still running (both would plan from the same data and, e.g., add the same clients twice). | Master MIS load clicked again while loading → refused | `JobIT.anImportCannotStartWhileTheSameImportIsStillRunning` |

## Other products: FD (entry)

| ID | Rule | Example | Proved by |
|---|---|---|---|
| R-70 | An FD's commission: gross = amount × margin %; TDS = gross × TDS % when ticked; GST = gross × GST %; net = gross − TDS; invoice = gross + GST; each rounded to paise. Worked out on every save and stored with the % used. | ₹10,00,000 at 1% margin, TDS 2%, GST 18% → gross 10,000; TDS 200; GST 1,800; net 9,800; invoice 11,800 | `FdRevenueTest.grossTdsGstNetAndInvoice`, `FdIT.anFdIsEnteredForANewClientAndItsRevenueWorkedOut` |
| R-71 | GST and TDS % are set **per product** on Setup → Products & taxes, from a date (earlier months keep the old rate). An entry takes the GST % of its month and starts with TDS ticked when the product says so. No rate for the month → the entry is refused. | FD GST 18% from Aug | `FdIT.taxRatesAreDatedPerProductAndAnEntryUsesItsMonthsRate` |
| R-72 | An FD's maturity value is worked out on save from its period in months. Cumulative: compounded n times a year (yearly 1 — the default —, half-yearly 2, quarterly 4) for each full period, simple interest on the months left over: P × (1 + r/n)^full × (1 + r × left/12). Interest paid out: P + P × r × months/12. Rounded to paise. Deposit date + months gives the maturity date (and back). | ₹5,00,000 at 7% for 33 months → 6,02,503.63 (register: 6,02,504); ₹1,00,000 at 8% 12 months quarterly → 1,08,243.22 | `FdMaturityTest.matchesTheFdRegistersFiguresYearlyThenSimpleForLeftOverMonths`, `FdMaturityTest.cumulativeCompoundsAsChosenAndPayoutAddsSimpleInterest` |
| R-73 | The FD form suggests what earlier FDs used: FD companies, platforms, and the margin last used for the same company + platform + period. The month downloads as Excel in the FD register's column order; client type = the client's code, or OFFLINE. | Bajaj Finance + J M Finance + 12 months → 0.77% offered | `FdIT.anFdIsEnteredForANewClientAndItsRevenueWorkedOut` |
| R-74 | Processing a month also takes its active FD entries and bond deals (Bonds % for bonds; Bonds column, consideration as AUM, "Bond: name ISIN"): each FD becomes an FD ledger line (gross = invoice, GST, net = commission excl. | R-75 | A **platform** (Setup → Platforms) has dated brokerage terms: whether its brokerage already **includes GST**, the **TDS %** it deducts, and **Epsilon's share %** of the mark-up (100 = all).
